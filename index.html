04 / the absolutes

Most rules bend. A few never do.

A brief can lift almost anything, and when it does, that gets written down with the reason. A short list is never lifted by anyone, including the person paying: no invented proof, no unreadable contrast, and nothing ships that has not been looked at in a real render.

05 / the method

Refusing is only half of it.

Strip out the tells and you are left with something plain, which fails just as badly. So every choice gets decided instead of defaulted: the concept, the face, the palette, the bones, and the one moment worth building properly. Written down before anything is built.

06 / the ledger

And it is not allowed to repeat itself.

Every build is logged against the axes that make a look: the ground, the face, the bones, the motion, the signature. The next one has to move on all of them. The fastest way to look machine made is to look exactly like the last thing you made.
