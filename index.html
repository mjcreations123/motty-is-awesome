08 / the ledger field

Every build is logged, and the next one has to go somewhere else.

The ledger records the axes that make a look: the ground, the face, the bones, the motion, the signature. The next build reads it and is not allowed to repeat them. It started empty and it was never backfilled, because a borrowed face is the fastest way to look machine made.
